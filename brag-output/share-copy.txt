SECTION 2: LINKEDIN / HACKER NEWS (Technical Launch Showcase)
================================================================================
In multi-branch universities, managing library collections across separate campus locations introduces complex concurrency challenges: when hundreds of students and faculty simultaneously request scarce physical copies across branches, standard ORM writes frequently lead to phantom reservations, lost inventory, and race conditions.

To solve this, I designed and built the Campus Library Management System—a full-stack, enterprise-grade platform engineered with Java 21, Spring Boot 3.2, Spring Security with stateless JWT authentication, and an interactive modern web frontend.

Key Architectural Highlights:
1. Concurrency Safety with Pessimistic Locking:
All physical book copy claims utilize JPA `@Lock(LockModeType.PESSIMISTIC_WRITE)` with strict query timeouts to ensure deterministic serialization across simultaneous checkout attempts.

2. Multi-Branch Campus Transit & Courier Logistics:
Spans Central Campus, CS Turing Stacks, and Franklin Mechanical Hall. Inter-branch transfers are powered by an automated 4-stage courier state engine: PENDING_COURIER ➔ IN_TRANSIT ➔ ARRIVED_AT_DESTINATION ➔ READY_FOR_PICKUP.

3. Automated 48-Hour Hold Lifecycle:
When a requested book reaches the destination branch desk, a resilient 48-hour pickup reserve window begins, backed by an automated Spring scheduler that releases expired holds.

4. Role-Segregated Workflows:
Role-based workflows tailored for Students (self-service discovery, transfer requests, real-time transit stepper), Faculty (extended loan privileges), and Librarians (rapid barcode scanner desk, instant check-in/out, transfer manifest handling).
